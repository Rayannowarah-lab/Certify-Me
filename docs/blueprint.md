# **App Name**: CertsHub

## Core Features:

- Certification Discovery Homepage: A modern homepage displaying a search bar, sections for major categories, and a curated list of featured certifications to help students begin their search.
- Major Category Browsing: Dedicated pages for browsing certifications categorized by university major or career field (e.g., Cyber Security, Data Science, Marketing).
- Certification Details View: Upon selection, a dedicated page showing comprehensive details for each certification including title, description, skills, organization, duration, difficulty, and a direct link.
- Advanced Search & Filtering: Enables users to search certifications by keyword and filter results efficiently by major and certification duration.
- Direct Certification Access: Each certification card and detail page provides a prominent 'Start Certification' button that links directly to the official certification enrollment or information page.
- Local JSON Data Storage: Utilizes a local 'certifications.json' file to store and retrieve all certification data, ensuring a simple and free-to-run backend-less operation.
- Pre-populated Certification Catalog: Includes a minimum of 30 real-world example certifications from reputable providers like Google, Microsoft, and IBM to kickstart the platform's utility.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color, signifying professionalism and educational value, is a deep, composed blue: #2D4F86 (RGB 45, 79, 134).
- The background color, providing a clean and inviting canvas, is a very light, almost white, blue-grey: #F1F3F8 (RGB 241, 243, 248).
- The accent color, for highlighting calls to action and key interactive elements, is a bright yet soothing sky blue: #6ECADE (RGB 110, 202, 222).
- For headlines and emphasized text, the app uses 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), chosen for its modern, slightly technical yet clean aesthetic.
- Body text and descriptive content will utilize 'Inter' (sans-serif), selected for its excellent readability and neutral, objective presentation.
- Icons should be clean, line-based, and minimalistic to align with the modern UI. Libraries such as Lucide or Heroicons are recommended for their clarity and scalability.
- Implement a responsive card grid layout for displaying certifications, ensuring optimal viewing across desktop and mobile devices, alongside clear and consistent spacing for all elements.
- Incorporate subtle hover effects on certification cards and smooth transitions for filters and search results to enhance user interaction without distraction.
